## Eligibility and weight

Eligibility and voting weight are public, resolved by the ballot's committed strategy: either
token voting power at the committed snapshot (IVotes) or a weighted allowlist whose Merkle
leaves bind chain, host, snapshot, your wallet, and your weight. Your weight is **fixed at
your first cast** — later token movements do not change it.

## Casting

You choose For, Against, or Abstain. The SDK sends the encoded choice to the attested Handle
Gateway, which encrypts it and returns the handle and proof your wallet transaction carries:

```ts
const { handle, handleProof } = await handleClient.encryptInput(choice, 'uint16', core)
await core.castVote(ballotId, sequence, handle, handleProof, eligibilityProof)
```

The public transaction contains the opaque handle — never your choice. Your operation then
moves **Submitted → Computing → Recorded**, and the receipt you get back confirms *status*,
not your plaintext choice. No progress percentage is ever shown, because none is knowable.

Every representable input is a defined choice: any value other than the canonical
For/Against/Abstain encodings is deterministically normalized to **Abstain**. There is no
hidden "invalid ballot" class that could leak information later.

Before close you may replace your vote up to **two times**, in strict sequence. The newest
accepted operation is the one that counts; the encrypted accounting removes your previous
contribution and adds the new one without either becoming public. Replacement is a
coercion-recovery window — the fact and timing of your replacement are public, its content is
not.

## What counts toward the privacy floor

Unique eligible wallets whose **newest effective operation is Recorded**. Abstain counts.
Replacements do not add a second participant. Pending, failed, rejected, and stale operations
do not count. You always see participation progress against the floor — never a running
tally, never a projected winner.
