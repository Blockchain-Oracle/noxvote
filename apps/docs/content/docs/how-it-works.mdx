Every ballot operation moves through five stages. What becomes public at each step: your
wallet, your voting weight, and the time you acted. Your choice is not among them, at any
stage.

## The five stages

1. **Preparing encrypted handle** — the SDK sends your encoded choice to the attested Handle
   Gateway, which encrypts it and returns a handle and proof. The Gateway sees the input; this
   is inside the declared trust boundary, and we say so plainly.
2. **Ready for wallet** — the transaction is assembled, waiting for your signature.
3. **Submitting** — the transaction goes on-chain. It contains the opaque handle, not your
   choice. Your wallet and the time are now public.
4. **Confidential computation** — Nox folds the operation into the sealed totals. No progress
   figure is shown, because none is knowable.
5. **Recorded** — a receipt confirms the operation status. Only an eligible wallet's newest
   effective Recorded operation counts.

## Replacement, floor, and the reveal

Before close you may replace your vote up to two times (`sequence` 1 → 2 → 3). The newest
accepted operation counts; a replacement never increases the unique-wallet count. Abstain is a
real participation choice, and any non-canonical encrypted value is deterministically
normalized to Abstain — there is no hidden validity class.

At close, anyone may request the tally. Two rules then apply, in order:

- Below the **privacy floor** (unique Recorded wallets), the result is **withheld** —
  terminally, with no Nox call and no verdict handle at all.
- At or above it, the core derives exactly one encrypted boolean — governance quorum met and
  For weight above Against weight — and grants public decryption to that handle **only**.

The contract accepts the configured Gateway's signed evidence for the exact stored verdict
handle, validates encoding, domain, and replay, and finalizes **Passed** or **Rejected** once.
Exact totals are not disclosed. Ever.
