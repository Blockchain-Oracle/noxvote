NoxVote turns on confidential voting for sensitive governance. Members' wallets and
participation stay public, their For/Against/Abstain choices never appear on-chain or become
public, and only the final Passed/Rejected verdict is revealed — after enough eligible wallets
recorded operations. A Passed verdict can execute the exact Safe or Governor action everyone
reviewed before voting.

It is not a standalone DAO app. It is a **host-neutral confidential ballot core** with two
production adapters: a Safe module (retrofit an existing Safe with a normal owner-threshold
transaction) and a compatible OpenZeppelin Governor (native governance-framework integration
with a real TimelockController).

## Public and private, exactly

| Always public | Never public |
| --- | --- |
| Your wallet address | Your choice |
| Your voting weight | Anyone else's choice |
| That you participated, and when | Exact option totals |
| Operation status (Submitted / Computing / Recorded) | Intermediate encrypted values |
| The committed action, rules, and dates | — |
| The final verdict (above the privacy floor) | — |

Two rules are committed before voting opens and never merged:

- The **privacy floor** decides whether *any* result may be disclosed at all. Below it, the
  outcome is **Result withheld** — no verdict, no totals, no manual release, nothing executes.
  That is a designed outcome, not a failure.
- The **governance quorum** decides whether the proposal passes, following the host's rule.
