## Story promise

The film makes Nox the privacy destination for DAO decisions, then proves that idea with NoxVote's
real Safe, Governor, Nox, and Sepolia path. The DAO does not migrate: its public governance host and
execution remain intact while confidential choice preparation and tally use Nox.

## Truth boundary

- The Handle Gateway is shown and named as a trust boundary; the film does not claim browser-only
  encryption.
- Participation remains public; the film does not claim voter anonymity.
- Nox is confidential TEE compute, not zero knowledge or FHE.
- Below the privacy floor, no aggregate is disclosed.
- The live proof claim is limited to the completed 2026-08-01 Sepolia checkpoint: `37/37`
  transactions, two real released-Nox verdicts, and both exact targets executed once.
